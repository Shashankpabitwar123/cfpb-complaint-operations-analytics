# Source notes

## Official source

- CFPB Consumer Complaint Database: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/consumer-complaints/
- Full CSV download used: https://files.consumerfinance.gov/ccdb/complaints.csv.zip
- Retrieval date: 2026-07-26

The downloaded source profile found records from 2011-12-01 through 2026-07-26. Because 2026 is incomplete at retrieval, the comparable analysis window is restricted to 2023–2025.

## Interpretation cautions

The CFPB states that the database is not a statistical sample and that complaint counts should not be interpreted as market-share-adjusted quality. Recent data may be incomplete because records are published after a company response/relationship confirmation or after 15 days. Findings in this project should therefore be described as observed complaint workload and response-record patterns.
